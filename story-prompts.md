# Children's Educational Story Prompts

## Story 1: The Helpful Little Boy

### Purpose
Teach children kindness, helping others, and caring for animals.

### Story
A little boy wearing a cream kurta and a white prayer cap is walking in a beautiful garden. He sees a small bird sitting on the ground with an injured wing. The boy gently asks an adult for help. Together, they care for the bird. Soon, the bird feels better and flies happily into the sky.

### AI Video Prompt
Create a high-quality 3D animated children's educational video in vertical 9:16 format. Show a kind little boy wearing a cream kurta pajama and a white prayer cap in a colorful garden. He notices a small injured bird, carefully asks an adult for help, and watches the bird recover. Use warm sunlight, expressive characters, gentle camera movement, and a peaceful atmosphere. Keep the same boy's appearance consistent throughout the scene.

### Urdu Voiceover
"ایک دن ایک نیک بچے نے باغ میں ایک زخمی پرندہ دیکھا۔ اس نے فوراً مدد طلب کی اور پرندے کا خیال رکھا۔ اللہ تعالیٰ نیکی کرنے والوں کو پسند فرماتا ہے۔"

### Moral
Always be kind to animals and help those in need.
